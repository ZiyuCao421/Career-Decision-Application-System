# Knowledge categories / 知识分类

- Profile: confirmed skills, projects, preferences, and resume-version labels.
- Applications: role facts, decision status, and follow-up chronology.
- Companies: legal/hiring entities, evidence, and risk flags.
- Market: dated role and skills observations by region.
- Interviews: events, feedback, and user reflections.
- Analytics: derived, dated summaries that can be traced to source records.
