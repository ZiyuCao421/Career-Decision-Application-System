# V1.0 evaluation matrix / 评价矩阵

## Layer 1 — application strategy / 求职策略层

State the active phase before interpreting any score: `Broad Application`, `Focused Application`, `Interview Conversion`, or `Offer Decision`.

In `Broad Application` (海投), the decision priority is **first-interview efficiency**. Scores support preparation and later comparison; they are not automatic rejection rules. A role may be retained with partial research unless its safety assessment shows an explicit high-risk signal.

Rate each assessed dimension from **1 (very weak/unfavourable)** to **5 (very strong/favourable)**. Use `N/A` when it cannot yet be assessed; never treat `N/A` as zero.

## Opportunity Score (independent)

| Dimension | Weight | Rating (1–5 or N/A) |
|---|---:|---|
| Company Stability | 20 | |

`Opportunity Score = Σ(weight × rating / 5)`, calculated only across assessed dimensions. Always state **coverage**: assessed weight / 100. Example: `62/75 assessed (83% coverage)`; do not present an incomplete score as fully precise.

## Risk flags

Risk flags are not part of either score. Record a flag, supporting evidence, its status (`Open`, `Verified`, `Resolved`), and next action. Examples: unverifiable hiring entity, upfront payment request, unclear contract entity, inconsistent salary promise, or material judicial/operating issue needing verification. A high-risk flag must remain visible regardless of score.

## Layer 4 — Job Safety Assessment / 岗位安全评估

This is a **separate assessment, not a third weighted score**. Its purpose is to prevent safety or compliance concerns from being averaged away by opportunity or fit. Use one status:

| Status | Meaning | Default action |
|---|---|---|
| `Not Assessed` | Evidence has not been checked. | Keep in pool during broad application; verify if progressing. |
| `Needs Verification` | Material evidence is incomplete, mixed, or only anonymous. | Continue only with a defined verification action. |
| `Clear on checked items` | No material concern found in the checked evidence. | Not a guarantee of safety; retain scope and date. |
| `High Concern` | A credible material risk flag is open or verified. | Pause or escalate before investing further. |

Check, where applicable: hiring and contract entity, official/credible business-operation signals, public credit or judicial concerns, upfront-payment requests, salary/role consistency, and job-source authenticity. “No issue found” may only mean the checked sources revealed none; it is never proof that no risk exists.
