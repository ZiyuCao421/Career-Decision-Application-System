# 30-minute company & job investigation checklist / 企业与岗位调查工卡

Create evidence records in the relevant application `investigation.md`. Mark every item as `Verified Fact`, `Unverified Information`, `Personal Opinion`, or `Rumour`.

## A. Basic information (5 minutes)

- Legal name, registration number, registered address, establishment date, company status.
- Region-specific details where applicable: Mainland China legal representative / registered capital; Singapore UEN.
- Actual hiring entity and proposed employment-contract entity.

## B. Operations & compliance (7 minutes)

- Main business, business scope, development signals.
- Public abnormal-operation, serious-violation, or judicial information, only from appropriate public sources.
- Note missing evidence as unverified; absence of findings is not evidence of no risk.

## C. Recruitment authenticity (5 minutes)

- Job source, original job URL, responsibilities, location, employment type, salary range, and recruiter details if voluntarily provided.
- Does the role plausibly match the company's business? Record evidence, not assumptions.

## D. Employee & social feedback (8 minutes)

- Record recurring themes about management, pay, workload, and hours.
- Separate attributable accounts, anonymous feedback, and rumours. Do not generalise a single review as fact.

## E. Decision (5 minutes)

- Overall risk level: `Low Risk`, `Needs Observation`, or `High Risk`.
- Risk flags, evidence summary, unverified items, and recommended next action.

## Evidence minimum

| Field | Required value |
|---|---|
| Source | Publisher, registry, employer, or platform |
| URL | Direct source link where available |
| Date Checked | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| Fact Summary | Concise, factual statement |
| Confidence | High / Medium / Low |
| Notes | Scope, limitations, or conflicting evidence |
